Root-only support-transfer pilot (2026-10-28)

Purpose: retain the existing GVHMR f4 articulation, fixed body shape and
same-frame interankle separation while comparing (1) original root,
(2) temporal-only root fitting, (3) temporal + support-transfer root fitting.
The latter has independent lead arrival/rear departure, marker-specific heel/toe
confidence, possible overlap, no hard XYZ anchor and one fixed plane per
candidate. Three uncalibrated plane-normal hypotheses survive in metadata.
No contact is applied when landing/side is unknown. This estimates kinematic
support hypotheses, not measured weight transfer, force or center of pressure.

The existing f4 camera is held fixed as a controlled root-only experiment.
This does not resolve camera/depth ambiguity. Joint orientations and per-frame
ankle spacing remain unchanged; cross-time displacement and absolute pitch
stride are not validated by that invariant. Plane/slip/smoothness costs are
part of fitting and cannot independently establish greater 3D accuracy.

Six existing tuning clips were piloted; the same setting then ran on the
remaining six of the already examined tuning12 subset. The original final
test24 and the other twelve tuning records were not used by this pilot.
This confirmation is not a new untouched final evaluation.

From the preserved research worktree (inputs remain outside the public repo):
  ../env/bin/python experiments/pitch_observation/support_transfer_root.py --cohort pilot
  ../env/bin/python experiments/pitch_observation/support_transfer_root.py --cohort confirmation
  ../env/bin/python experiments/pitch_observation/view_support_transfer.py

Results are ../support_transfer_root_v1/. The same commands resume cached fits;
changed configs/input hashes are rejected and require a new versioned output
directory. The local numerical-only viewer is comparison/index.html.
No public Explorer models, final Halpe-26 files or canonical index are changed.

Observed over tuning12 (clip medians):
                            original f4   temporal root   + support transfer
  root accel p95, leg/s^2        109.08          18.68              20.19
  assumed-contact speed leg/s      1.64           1.13               0.47
  source reprojection p50 px       2.57           2.72               3.05
  wrist reprojection p95 px        4.50           4.91               4.75
Contact metrics use only the ten clips with contact hypotheses. Same-frame
interankle changes are below 6e-16 model leg units; poses are unchanged.
The preset +2px source-median/wrist-p95 regression screen passes 12/12, but is
an experiment screen, not a source visual review or a 3D accuracy claim.

Interpretation: most root stability comes from temporal regularization;
support transfer further reduces assumed-contact motion. Source fitting gets
slightly worse. All ten contact cases choose the boundary plane-normal seed
(camera-Y-up); ground geometry and camera sensitivity need further checks.
Do not deploy as validated global motion or expand merely because these
objective-linked consistency metrics improved.

Why the public archive has only 36 new results:
fixed48 = tune24 + test24; executed GVHMR = tune12 + test24; NLF = tune12 +
test12. The exporter iterates the 36-record viewer catalog. The full813
reconstruction runner has not been run; 777 clips still use preserved WHAM.
36 is the executed experiment coverage, not a count of validated successes.
